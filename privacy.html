Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Kritic is a movie rating app made by Frank Nowinski ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and, if you allow it, your name and Google profile photo. We never see your password.
  • Profile: the name, username and optional photo you confirm, and whether your profile is private.
  • Your activity in Kritic: your ratings and scores, optional one-line reviews, your watchlist, movies you mark as seen, haven't seen or not interested, the streaming services you pick, the people you follow and who follows you, and your answers to "which did you like more?".
  • Letterboxd import: if you import a Letterboxd export, the file is read on your phone and never uploaded. Only the ratings, watched films and watchlist it contains are saved to your Kritic account.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, the screen you were on, your device model, iOS and app version, and your account ID (never your name or email) is sent to our crash reporting service.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile, ratings and lists.
  • To work out your picks, your "You'd like it" %, and taste matches with people you follow.
  • To show Kritic-wide numbers such as a movie's Kritic average or what's popular this week. These are counts and averages; they never name you.
  • To find and fix bugs.

Who can see what

  • Your name, username and photo are public to other Kritic users, and your profile photo is stored at a public web address.
  • Your ratings and reviews can be seen by other users unless your profile is private; then only followers you approve can see them.
  • Movie searches are sent from our server to TMDB (The Movie Database) without anything that identifies you.

Service providers

We use a few companies to run Kritic. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage (servers in the United States).
  • Sentry: crash reporting.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete account: this permanently deletes your profile, photos, ratings, reviews, watchlist and follows. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. To get a copy of your data, email us.

Children

Kritic is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
