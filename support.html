Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email kritic.app@gmail.com and include your username and what you were doing. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Settings (the gear on your Profile) › Delete account. It's permanent.

How do I make my ratings private?
Settings › Private profile. New followers then need your approval.

How do I import from Letterboxd?
Export your data from letterboxd.com (Settings › Import & Export), then in Kritic go to Settings › Import from Letterboxd and pick the .zip.

Why don't I see a "You'd like it" %?
Rate at least 10 movies. The more you rate, the better your picks get.
